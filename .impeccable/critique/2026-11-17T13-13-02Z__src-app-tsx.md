## Cognitive Load

Grouping passes through columns, bordered cards, and dividers. Single focus, hierarchy, and progressive disclosure fail as expanded history grows. Chunking and simultaneous card decisions worsen at scale. Working-memory cost rises when the goal is offscreen and actual criteria require Edit. Five coherent statuses should remain; arbitrary numeric choice limits are not a reason to remove domain categories. The demo filter has six options and status selectors have five, which are decision points to structure clearly rather than suppress.

## Emotional Journey

Projects and demo entry are calm and approachable. Opening the populated project produces a valley: a historical document appears before actionable work. Completion restores clarity by explaining outcomes and carryover. Saved reflections then accumulate before the next sprint's work. Your data provides strong reassurance, though ordinary project work does not foreground where data lives.

## Persona Red Flags

- Returning solo developer: scrolls through historical reflections to reach current work; repeated per-card controls create administrative effort.
- First-time planner: discovers the one-active-sprint rule after clicking Start sprint; must distinguish Backlog status from unassigned work and understand review/retro terms.
- Phone user: small New project/Edit targets; source stacks statuses from Backlog through Done, potentially delaying current work. Phone-board behavior needs live verification.

## Minor Observations

- Consider read-only disclosure of descriptions and criteria rather than requiring Edit merely to inspect them.
- Project rows lack a concise active-goal or current-work cue.
- The Projects navigation item is not active on project detail, though a breadcrumb supplies location.
- Demo data interrupts Export and Import on Your data.
- Neutral color suits this utility. Stronger hierarchy is more valuable than a new palette.

## Questions to Consider

- Should the project open on active sprint work, all-project work, or a compact overview?
- Should the next pass prioritize daily-work hierarchy, phone usability, or both with visual polish?
